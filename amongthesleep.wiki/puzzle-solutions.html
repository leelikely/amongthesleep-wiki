All Puzzle Solutions

Stuck somewhere in the dark? You're not alone. Among the Sleep hides its puzzles in plain sight — a key perched on a cabinet too tall to reach, a bookshelf that hides a secret crawlspace, a set of tokens you need to unlock a cabin door. Below you'll find every puzzle in the game, broken down by chapter, with clear step-by-step solutions. No spoilers beyond what you need. Pick your chapter and get unstuck.

Chapter 1

How to Get the Nursery Key

Difficulty ★☆☆☆☆ Location The Nursery

The first puzzle of the game is deceptively simple. You've just tumbled out of the crib, Teddy is in your arms, and the nursery door is locked. The key sits on top of a tall cabinet — far beyond a toddler's reach. You'll need to bridge the height gap using the only movable object in the room.

  1. Locate the small wooden chair near the play area (by the building blocks).
  2. Walk into the chair to push it toward the tall cabinet across the room.
  3. Approach the chair from the front — a climbing animation will trigger.
  4. From the chair, look up at the cabinet top and interact to grab the key.
  5. Walk to the nursery door and interact to unlock it.
💡 Pro Tip

Grab the hidden drawing from inside the wardrobe before unlocking the door. Once you walk through, Chapter 1 ends and you can't go back without replaying.

Chapter 2

Kitchen Cabinet Puzzle

Difficulty ★★☆☆☆ Location The Kitchen

The kitchen is massive from a toddler's perspective, and the locked cabinet that leads to the next area requires a key. The key sits on the countertop — impossible to reach from the floor. The solution: build a staircase out of kitchen chairs.

  1. Locate all three wooden chairs in the kitchen (center, near fridge, behind table).
  2. Push each chair toward the counter where the key sits — cluster them close together.
  3. Climb onto the first chair, then the second, then the third — forming a staircase.
  4. From the highest chair, reach for the counter ledge and pull yourself up.
  5. Grab the cabinet key (and the sippy cup for a memory fragment).
  6. Climb down and use the key on the locked cabinet near the pantry door.
💡 Chairs Not Stacking?

Push the chairs so they're touching. The game reads them as a chain — gaps break the climb sequence. Approach each chair from the front to trigger the animation.

Chapter 2

Stacked Chairs Puzzle

Difficulty ★★★☆☆ Location The Kitchen

This is the same puzzle as above — the "stacked chairs" and "kitchen cabinet" are two descriptions of the same challenge. But there's nuance. The darkness sequence after you grab the key turns this from a simple stacking exercise into a tense navigation puzzle. Once the lights die, you need to find the cabinet in pitch black using Teddy's glow and the refrigerator's hum as an audio beacon.

  1. After the lights go out, hug Teddy — his glow is your only light source.
  2. Listen for the refrigerator's hum — it's loudest when facing the cabinet wall.
  3. Move slowly toward the hum. The cabinet is on the same wall as the pantry door.
  4. Ignore the shadowy figure at the edge of Teddy's light — it's scripted and harmless.
  5. Reach the cabinet door and interact to unlock it with the key.
ℹ️ Darkness Tip

This is the game's first darkness navigation test. Future chapters will ask more of you. Master the "hug Teddy, move slow, follow audio" loop now.

Chapter 3

Living Room Escape — Survive and Solve

Difficulty ★★★☆☆ Location The Living Room

This is the first chapter where the shadow creature actively hunts you. You enter the living room, and within seconds it spawns behind you. Your goal: survive the chase, then solve the bookshelf puzzle to escape — all while the creature patrols the room.

  1. Chase phase: Sprint to the sofa and crouch underneath. Stay hidden until the creature retreats.
  2. Observation phase: From under the sofa, watch the creature's patrol cycle — it moves between the doorway, fireplace, and far corner every 15–20 seconds.
  3. Action phase: When the creature is at the far corner, move to the fallen bookshelf against the far wall.
  4. Near the base of the bookshelf, find the book with the red cover. Push it aside.
  5. A crawlspace is revealed. Crouch and crawl through before the creature's next patrol.
🚫 Do Not Release Teddy

Letting go of Teddy during the chase slows you down and makes the creature more aggressive. Hold onto him at all costs.

Chapter 4

Hallway Chase — Surviving the Shadow

Difficulty ★★★★☆ Location The Hallway

The hardest sequence in the game. You must navigate a 90-meter hallway divided into five segments, each with its own hiding spot. The shadow creature patrols continuously. At the end, you must get close enough to steal a key from around its neck — then sprint to the door before it catches you.

  1. Segment 1: Hide in the broom closet (right wall). Wait for the creature to pass.
  2. Segment 2: Hide behind the overturned armoire (left wall). Tight timing — 15 seconds between passes.
  3. Segment 3: Crawl under the console table (right wall). The chandelier barrier means the creature won't follow past this point.
  4. Segment 4: Hide behind the fallen painting (left wall). Freeze during the creature's 8-second scan.
  5. Segment 5: Crawl inside the cardboard box (right wall). Wait for the creature to move toward the portrait gallery.
  6. Key grab: Approach the creature from behind during its scan. Take the key. Sprint to the door.
💡 Audio Strategy

Play with headphones. The creature has three distinct audio ranges: low thrum (<15m, danger), dragging (15–30m, safe to move), silence (>30m, safe to advance).

Chapter 5

Forest Final Puzzle — The Four Tokens

Difficulty ★★★☆☆ Location The Forest / Cabin Clearing

After navigating the dark forest (follow the amber light, the trail of objects, and the humming), you arrive at a log cabin. The door is locked. Beside it: a wooden box with four slots — bear, tree, moon, house. Four matching tokens are hidden around the clearing. Find them all to enter the cabin and complete the game.

  1. Bear Token: At the base of a large pine tree on the left side of the clearing.
  2. Tree Token: Hanging from a low branch on the right side of the clearing.
  3. Moon Token: Sitting on a flat rock near the cabin's front step — easy to miss.
  4. House Token: Partially buried in the pine needles directly in front of the cabin door.
  5. Insert all four tokens into their matching slots. Order doesn't matter.
  6. The box chimes, the door opens. Your mother is inside.
💡 Before You Enter

Circle around to the back of the cabin before going inside. There's a woodpile — beside it, the final hidden collectible drawing is pinned to the cabin wall. This is your last chance to grab it.
