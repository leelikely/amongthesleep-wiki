Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026. We take your privacy seriously. Here's exactly what we do and don't do with your data.

1. Introduction

Welcome to Among the Sleep Guide (amongthesleep.wiki). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect any information you provide while using our website. We are committed to complying with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) to the extent applicable.

What This Policy Covers

  • What data we collect automatically when you visit
  • How we use cookies and similar technologies
  • Advertising partners and data sharing
  • Your rights and how to contact us

2. Information We Collect

Automatically Collected Data

When you visit our website, certain information is automatically logged by our server and third‑party services. This may include:

  • Your IP address (anonymized where possible)
  • Browser type and version
  • Operating system
  • Referring URL (the page that linked to us)
  • Pages visited and time spent
  • Device screen size and type (for responsive design)

This information is used solely for analytics (e.g., Google Analytics) and to improve our content. It does not identify you personally unless combined with other data.

Data You Provide Voluntarily

We do not have user accounts or comments. The only personal data we may receive is when you choose to contact us via our Contact page or email. This may include your name and email address, which we use exclusively to reply to your message. We never sell, rent, or share this information with third parties for their own purposes.

3. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Like most modern websites, we use cookies and similar technologies to enhance your experience. Cookies are small text files stored on your device. We use them for:

  • Essential functionality: Remembering your cookie consent choice (so we don't ask again).
  • Analytics: Understanding how visitors use our guide (aggregated, anonymized data).
  • Advertising: Serving relevant ads through our advertising partner, Monetag.

When you first visit, a cookie consent banner appears. You can choose to accept or decline non‑essential cookies. If you decline, we only set the essential cookie to remember your choice; no tracking or advertising cookies will be placed. You can also clear your cookies at any time through your browser settings.

4. Advertising Partners

We display advertisements to support the free content on this guide. Our ad partner, Monetag, may use cookies and similar tracking technologies to deliver personalized or contextual ads. Monetag acts as a data processor on our behalf and is subject to its own privacy policy.

Monetag's services may involve:

  • In‑page push notifications (with your consent)
  • Vignette (interstitial) ads
  • Standard banner advertising
  • Interest‑based profiling (where permitted by law)

We do not control these third‑party cookies or tracking. To learn more and opt out, please visit YourAdChoices or the European Interactive Digital Advertising Alliance.

5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal data. We may share anonymized or aggregated data with:

  • Analytics providers (e.g., Google Analytics) to monitor website performance.
  • Advertising partners (e.g., Monetag) to deliver ads, as described above.
  • Legal authorities if required by law or to protect our rights.

These parties are obligated to use your data only in accordance with our instructions and applicable data protection laws.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: Request a copy of the data we hold about you.
  • Rectification: Ask us to correct any inaccurate information.
  • Erasure: Request deletion of your data (subject to legal obligations).
  • Objection: Object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Data portability: Receive your data in a structured, machine‑readable format.
  • Withdraw consent: Change your cookie preferences at any time by clearing your browser cookies and revisiting our site.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at privacy@amongthesleep.wiki. We will respond within 30 days.

7. Children's Privacy

Our website is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately so we can remove it.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review it periodically.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please reach out via our Contact page or directly at privacy@amongthesleep.wiki.
