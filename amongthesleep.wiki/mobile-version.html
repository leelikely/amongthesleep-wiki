Is the Mobile Version the Same as PC/Console?

Yes. The Among the Sleep mobile version is the complete, uncut game — identical in content to the PC, PlayStation, Xbox, and Nintendo Switch versions. Every chapter, every puzzle, every hidden collectible drawing, both endings, and all memory fragments are fully included with no content removed or simplified for mobile.

Krillbite Studio has been explicit: the mobile port of Among the Sleep is not a "mobile version" in the compromised sense that term often implies. It is the full Enhanced Edition of the game, re-engineered for touchscreen devices. All five chapters — from the nursery to the forest — are present and complete. The shadow creature still stalks you through the living room and hallway. The hidden drawings are still tucked into wardrobes, behind cabinets, and under burlap sacks. Both endings are unlockable on mobile under the same conditions as PC and console.

The only differences between the mobile and PC/console versions are the control scheme (touch vs. keyboard/mouse or gamepad) and minor graphical optimizations for mobile hardware. The art direction, lighting, audio design, and narrative are untouched. If you've played Among the Sleep on another platform, the mobile version is the same experience. If you haven't, you'll get the full game — no asterisks.

Touch Controls — How Will Among the Sleep Work on Mobile?

The transition from keyboard/mouse or gamepad to a touchscreen is the single biggest question mark for any premium PC-to-mobile port, and Krillbite has clearly put significant thought into getting it right. Based on developer previews and our analysis of the control scheme, here's what to expect.

Virtual Joystick + Contextual Touch

The mobile version uses a virtual joystick positioned on the lower-left portion of the screen for character movement. On the lower-right, contextual touch buttons appear based on what's happening in the game: an interact button when near objects, a dedicated hug button (a Teddy icon) for holding the bear close, and a sprint toggle for chase sequences. The layout is minimal — the majority of the screen remains clear for the game's atmospheric visuals. Double-tapping replaces the single-click interaction from PC, which Krillbite implemented specifically to prevent accidental interactions while navigating.

How Touch Controls Affect the Horror Experience

There's an interesting tension here. On one hand, touch controls are slightly less precise than a mouse and keyboard — which, in a horror game where clumsiness and vulnerability are intentional design themes, arguably enhances the experience. The toddler's movements are meant to feel awkward and imprecise, and touch controls align with that naturally. On the other hand, chase sequences (particularly in Chapters 3 and 4) require quick reactions, and mobile players may find the sprint button placement takes a few attempts to build muscle memory for. Our chapter-specific mobile notes in the full walkthrough cover these moments in detail.

Controller Support

Bluetooth controller support is expected at launch. Krillbite has confirmed they are optimizing the experience for both touch and controller input. If you prefer a traditional gamepad experience — whether a Backbone One, Xbox controller, or PlayStation DualSense paired via Bluetooth — you'll likely be able to play Among the Sleep on mobile with the same control scheme you'd use on console.
