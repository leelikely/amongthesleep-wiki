All Collectibles Locations

Hidden throughout Among the Sleep are five child's drawings — crayon sketches that capture moments from the toddler's fractured world. Each one is tucked away in a corner most players walk right past. Find them all, and the game rewards you with the secret ending — an additional two-minute sequence that fundamentally reframes the story. Miss even one, and you'll only see the standard ending. Below: every drawing, every location, in the order you'll encounter them. Happy hunting.

ℹ️ Why Collect These?

All five drawings are required for the secret ending. The game allows chapter select from the main menu — you can replay individual chapters to grab missing drawings without restarting the entire game. Your progress is tracked automatically. See the interactive checklist at the bottom of this page to track what you've found.

Drawing #1 of 5

Nursery Drawing — Inside the Wardrobe

Chapter 1 — The Nursery ⚠️ Missable

The very first collectible in the game. The drawing is hidden inside the tall wooden wardrobe against the wall opposite the crib — the one with the hanging clothes that sway as if someone just brushed past them. Most players are so focused on reaching the key that they never bother to open the wardrobe at all.

Location & Steps

  1. After climbing out of the crib and picking up Teddy, walk to the wardrobe — it's the large wooden furniture directly across from the crib.
  2. Interact with the wardrobe doors to swing them open. The interior is dim; hug Teddy to illuminate it.
  3. Look at the back wall of the wardrobe, near the bottom (at toddler eye-level).
  4. The drawing is taped to the wood — a crayon sketch of a house with a single glowing window, a stick figure outside.
  5. Interact to collect it. The game will confirm with a subtle chime.
⚠️ Missable — Grab It Before You Leave

Once you unlock the nursery door and step into the hallway, Chapter 1 ends. You cannot return to the nursery. If you miss this drawing, you'll need to replay the chapter to get it. We recommend grabbing it before you solve the key puzzle — that way you won't get swept up in the exit transition and forget.

Drawing #2 of 5

Kitchen Drawing — Behind the Dining Room Cabinet

Chapter 2 — The Kitchen ⚠️ Missable

This one is sneaky — it's not in the kitchen itself, but in the dining room vestibule you pass through after crawling through the cabinet passage. Most players are so relieved to escape the dark kitchen that they sprint right through without looking around.

Location & Steps

  1. Complete the stacked chairs puzzle, survive the darkness sequence, and crawl through the cabinet passage.
  2. You'll emerge in the dining room vestibule — a small transitional space with a grandfather clock and a draped dining table.
  3. Against one wall is a tall wooden cabinet pushed slightly away from the wall — there's a gap behind it.
  4. Crouch (Ctrl) and squeeze into the gap.
  5. The drawing is taped to the back wall of the alcove, at floor level — a kitchen scene heavily scribbled over with black crayon, a tall dark figure in the corner.
⚠️ Missable — Easy to Sprint Past

The vestibule is small and unassuming. It takes maybe 15 seconds to walk through — and that's exactly why most players miss this. The gap behind the cabinet is shadowed and easy to overlook, especially if you're still shaken from the darkness sequence. Pause, breathe, check behind the cabinet.

Drawing #3 of 5

Living Room Drawing — Overturned Desk Drawer

Chapter 3 — The Living Room ⚠️ Missable

After surviving the shadow creature chase and crawling through the bookshelf gap, you'll find yourself in a small study — the only truly safe room in the chapter. This is where the collectible hides. The study feels like a sanctuary, and that sense of relief is exactly what makes players rush through without checking the drawers.

Location & Steps

  1. Navigate through the bookshelf crawlspace into the study room.
  2. In the study, find the overturned wooden desk — it's near the flickering desk lamp.
  3. One of the desk drawers has fallen out and lies on the floor beside the desk.
  4. Look inside the fallen drawer — the drawing is tucked at the back.
  5. The image: a tall dark figure looming over a small shape that might be a crib.
💡 Bonus Memory Fragment

While you're in the study, interact with the fallen desk lamp — it triggers a memory fragment that provides crucial story context for Chapters 4 and 5. This room has value beyond the collectible.

Drawing #4 of 5

Hallway Drawing — Twisted Corridor Alcove

Chapter 4 — The Hallway ⚠️ Missable

The fourth drawing is in the twisted corridor — the non-Euclidean space you navigate after the hallway chase. It's hidden behind a torn piece of wallpaper near the sideways staircase, and the surreal geometry of this section makes it genuinely easy to miss.

Location & Steps

  1. After unlocking the door and escaping the shadow creature, enter the twisted corridor.
  2. Walk for roughly one minute until you reach the sideways staircase — it runs horizontally along the left wall, ascending toward what used to be the ceiling.
  3. At the base of these stairs (floor level to your left), there's a small alcove partially hidden by torn wallpaper.
  4. Crouch and enter the alcove. The drawing is pinned to the back wall.
  5. The image: a long dark hallway with a tiny figure at one end and a tall scribbled shape at the other.
💡 Teddy Glow Proximity Indicator

As with other hidden objects in the game, Teddy's glow will brighten slightly when you're near the alcove. Hug Teddy and walk along the left wall — when the glow intensifies, you've found the spot.

Drawing #5 of 5

Forest Drawing — Behind the Cabin

Chapter 5 — The Forest ⚠️ Missable

The final collectible is the easiest to miss because it's located after the token puzzle but before you enter the cabin — a narrative sweet spot where most players are too emotionally invested to think about exploring the exterior. This drawing is the most detailed and hopeful of the entire set.

Location & Steps

  1. Collect all four tokens and insert them into the wooden box beside the cabin door — but do not enter the cabin yet.
  2. Circle around to the back of the cabin.
  3. You'll find a small woodpile stacked against the rear wall. Beside it, a burlap sack partially covers something.
  4. Move the sack aside. The final drawing is pinned to the cabin's back wall at toddler height.
  5. The image: a cabin in a forest, two figures (one large, one small) standing together under a silver moon. Unlike the others, this one isn't crossed out.
⚠️ Last Chance for All Drawings

If this is your final missing drawing and you've collected #1–#4, the secret ending will trigger automatically after the standard ending concludes. But if you enter the cabin without this drawing, you'll need to replay Chapter 5 — the door closes behind you.
